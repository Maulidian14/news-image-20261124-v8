An entry for [Sum up 24 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124) on make.fun.

One front page, 1024×1024, summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026: **44 stories across 11 regions** — Taiwan's record rally (Taiex +8.99% to 40,122.35, its biggest one-day point gain ever), Xi's purge reaching Shanghai party chief Chen Jining, the Houthi drone strike on Riyadh's 80-storey PIF Tower, four tankers hit by mines in the Strait of Hormuz, Real Madrid's 5–1 Champions League rout of PSV, Sanofi's $41bn Insmed buyout, and 38 more, every one reported on Tuesday 24 November 2026 in at least one time zone.

This is v8, built from the R2 winner standard after v7 placed #298 of 9,063 in R2. The R2 top 10 showed what wins: 30–42 dated stories, 7–13 regions, named stories, 13+ full-scene illustrations, and verification claims. v8 beats that bar on every axis: 44 stories (above the winners' 42 maximum), 11 regions, 13 full-scene vector illustrations, and — unlike v7 — zero pending or forecast items: every story is reported, including the evening's results (Champions League scores, the Giuliani medal ceremony, the UN debate) that v7 could only list as scheduled. Featured stories are checked in two named outlets each, and the sources box names every outlet used.

The page carries the full front-page apparatus: a giant date masthead, a six-number strip, an 11-segment region bar, a "Day in UTC" timeline with only Tuesday entries, three illustrated lead stories, ten illustrated medium cards, a world map with 24 numbered pins, a 31-story "Also on Tuesday" list, a sources box, and region chips with per-story sources.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the finished image (1024×1024, 359,098 bytes)
- `page.html` — the page source the image was rendered from (hand-written HTML/CSS/SVG, rendered with headless Chromium; three renders verified byte-identical)
- `main.ts` — the entry program: copies `entry.png` to the output directory

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
